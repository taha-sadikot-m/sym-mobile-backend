Privacy Policy

Speak Your Mind app · Last updated {{updated}}

Speak Your Mind is a practice app from SYM Education for Model United Nations and debate. This policy explains what we collect when you use the app, why, who helps us process it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. Your app account is the same account you use on the SYM website, so this policy also covers how the app shares your practice with the website.

If you are under 18, please read this with a parent or guardian and ask them before you create an account.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address, password (stored only as a secure one-way hash), and, if you choose to add them, a username, your age, school, city, country and MUN experience.
  • Your practice: the text of your speeches, answers and debate turns (transcribed from your voice or typed by you), the topics, committees and countries you chose, and the scores, feedback and reports the app produces.
  • Live debates with other students: the challenge details (topic, format, time), the transcript of each turn, the verdict, and your private performance report. Your opponent sees the verdict and the shared transcript, never your private report.
  • Safety records: reports you make about another student, reports made about you, and the students you block.
  • Device and usage: a notification token for your device if you allow notifications, the device type, and technical logs (such as when you signed in and from which network address) used to keep accounts secure.

Your voice recordings

When you speak, the recording is made on your phone and sent securely to our speech-to-text provider to be turned into text. We do not store your recordings: only the text is kept. In live debates, your voice is carried to your opponent in real time and is not recorded by us or by our audio provider.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your account, practice sessions, live debates, reminders and notifications.
  • To coach you: AI reads your transcripts to give feedback, ask Points of Information, act as your debate opponent, and judge live debates.
  • To show your progress in the app and on the SYM website (your debate history and Skill Ladder level).
  • To keep students safe: reviewing reports, and pausing accounts that break the rules.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to understand overall use (for example, how many sessions are completed). We do not sell your data or show you advertising.

Who helps us

We use these service providers, who process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Google (Gemini API) — AI feedback, questions, opponent and judging, using your transcripts.
  • Deepgram — turning your recordings into text.
  • LiveKit — carrying live debate voice between you and your opponent.
  • Neon (database) and Render (servers) — storing and running the service.
  • Resend — sending sign-in and password-reset codes by email.
  • Expo — delivering notifications to your phone through Apple and Google.

Some of these providers process data outside India. We may also share information when the law requires it, or to protect a student's safety.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and practice history: until you delete your account.
  • Email codes: deleted a day after they expire. Sign-in sessions: deleted 30 days after they end.
  • Notification records: 90 days. Records of AI requests (no transcripts, just usage): 400 days.
  • Voice recordings: never stored.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your details any time in Profile → Edit profile.
  • Notifications: turn them off in Profile, or in your phone's settings.
  • Block or report another student from their challenge or result screen.
  • Delete your account in Profile → Delete account. This deletes your profile, practice sessions, your turns in live debates and your private reports, empties your words from debate transcripts on the SYM website, and removes your personal details from your SYM account (which is shared with the website). Debate results your opponents took part in stay in their history with your name removed.
  • For any other request or question, contact us at {{support}}.

Security

Connections are encrypted, passwords are stored as one-way hashes, sign-in codes expire quickly and are limited in attempts, and access by SYM staff to student details is limited to the people who need it and recorded in an audit log.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
